## Neuroscience

The retina is not a passive camera — it computes. Retinal ganglion cells perform edge detection, motion tracking, and contrast enhancement before any signal reaches the brain. The magnocellular pathway handles fast motion (where), while the parvocellular pathway handles fine detail and color (what).

You sense far more than you consciously perceive. R1 is the raw data layer.
