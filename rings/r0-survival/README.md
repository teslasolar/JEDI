## Neuroscience

The enteric nervous system (ENS) contains ~500 million neurons — more than the spinal cord. It operates independently of the brain via local reflex circuits, producing gut feelings that travel up the vagus nerve to shape emotion and decision-making before conscious awareness.

90% of vagal fibers are afferent (gut→brain). Your gut literally informs your brain more than your brain instructs your gut.
