## Neuroscience

V4 is the color and form area — it learns what to see through experience. Expert birdwatchers have measurably different V4 activation than novices looking at the same bird. You see more of what you love.

Identity is the accumulation of learned perceptual patterns. What you identify with shapes what your visual cortex is tuned to recognize.
