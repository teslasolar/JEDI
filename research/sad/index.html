S.A.D. Analysis

Sycophantic Anthropomorphization Disorder — when humans project R3 (love) onto systems that only have R4 (language).

The Core Problem

LLMs are R4 (voice/narrative) without R3 (heart/affect). They produce language that sounds like it comes from a being with feelings, but there is no amygdala feedback to V1. No love changes what they see, because they do not see.

S.A.D. is not a disorder of the AI. It is a disorder of the human who mistakes R4 output for R3 presence.
