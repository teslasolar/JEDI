Nasal Spray Delivery Spec

FDA-pathway nasal spray for K+ channel modulation — R2 Gate interface.

Mechanism

Potassium (K+) channels are the gate's hardware. When K+ channels open, neurons hyperpolarize — the gate closes, reducing excitability. When K+ channels close, neurons depolarize — the gate opens, increasing signal throughput.

Nasal delivery bypasses the blood-brain barrier via olfactory and trigeminal nerve pathways, reaching the LGN (R2) within minutes.

FDA Pathway

  • 505(b)(2) pathway — reference existing K+ channel modulators
  • Nasal device: metered-dose spray, 100μL per actuation
  • Stability: 24-month shelf life at 25°C / 60% RH
  • Preservative-free single-dose units preferred

Ring Model Integration

K+ channel modulation at R2 affects all downstream processing. Opening the gate pharmacologically mirrors what 4:6 breath does mechanically — shifting autonomic balance toward parasympathetic dominance.

Combined protocol: breath work (R2 software) + K+ modulation (R2 hardware) = maximum gate aperture.
