Isaiah 14:12, Lucifer/Phosphorus/Venus, and Shingo honest scholarship on the morning star as light-bringer.

Etymology

Lucifer (Latin) = Phosphorus (Greek) = light-bringer = Venus = the morning star.

Isaiah 14:12 addresses the King of Babylon, not a cosmic entity. The conflation happened in translation. The morning star is just a star. Light is just light.
